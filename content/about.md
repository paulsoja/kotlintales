title: "About Me"
---

I'm Pavlo Soia, an Android developer with over 10 years of experience.

Over the years, I've become increasingly interested not only in how to implement features, but also in how to design systems that remain understandable, reliable, and easy to evolve over time.

That's how KotlinTales came to be. I started this blog primarily to organize my own knowledge, explore topics that interest me in greater depth, and share my experience with fellow developers.

Here, I write about software architecture, domain modeling, functional programming, type-driven design, and software engineering practices in general.

My goal is not to present universal solutions or claim that there is only one right way to do things. I'm more interested in exploring different approaches, understanding their strengths and limitations, and, most importantly, discovering why and under what circumstances they work.

I hope this blog becomes not only a place for my own reflections but also a space for sharing ideas and meaningful technical discussions.
